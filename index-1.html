THE ACTUAL BUSINESS PROBLEM

Three small leaks that can add up.

We do not sell vague automation promises. We measure a clinic's real follow-up process and focus on the part that loses bookings.

01

Delayed responses

Prospects ask after hours, contact multiple clinics and move on before a coordinator replies.

02

Incomplete follow-up

A prospective client asks for a price, gets an answer and the conversation quietly ends without a next step.

03

Unfilled time

Cancelled or unconfirmed appointments leave openings that are not always recovered.

OUR PROPOSED PILOT

Measure first. Recover second. Prove the difference.

Step one is a limited audit using approved operational data, followed by a human-reviewed follow-up workflow and a simple weekly outcome report.

WEEK 01

Map the leakage

Measure inbound volumes, response times and whether enquiries end with an actionable next step.

WEEK 02

Test one channel

Deploy a minimal approved workflow on one channel, with a staff handoff and no clinical advice.

REPORT

Report the facts

Show enquiries responded to, qualified, booked and actually attended — without claiming every conversion was caused by us.

IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

What makes this workable?

Does this replace the receptionist?

No. The pilot is a simple follow-up and alert layer. All clinical questions, pricing exceptions and appointment confirmation can be escalated to the clinic's staff.

Does the demo connect to WhatsApp or a clinic system?

No. This page is a standalone demonstration and scenario calculator. Live integrations would require separately approved accounts, a technical setup and a clinic agreement.

How is patient data handled?

A real pilot must follow applicable UAE privacy and healthcare information rules, minimize the personal information processed, document necessary consent and limit access. The demonstration does not collect patient data.

What happens if the pilot does not deliver?

We review actual audited metrics and stop or redesign; no outcome or refund policy is promised before both parties sign agreed pilot terms.
